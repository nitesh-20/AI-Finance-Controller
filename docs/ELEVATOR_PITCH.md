# AI Finance Controller — 30-Second Elevator Pitch
**Track 04: AI Finance Controller ("Run the books and the cash position")**  
**Razorpay AI Buildathon Submission**  
**Target Duration:** Exactly 30 seconds (~75 spoken words)

---

## Word-for-Word Pitch

> "Every growing business struggles with three-way financial reconciliation across payment gateways, bank accounts, and invoices—where a single bad match quietly corrupts the general ledger.
> 
> The **AI Finance Controller** solves this through one fundamental principle:  
> **AI proposes. Deterministic financial logic verifies. Human approvals control high-risk actions.**
> 
> In our held-out evaluation of 1,000 multi-source records, naive matchers silently posted 75 incorrect entries. Our controller achieved a **91% verified auto-match rate** with **100% verified auto-match precision** and **zero incorrect auto-posts observed**—turning unresolved risk into an honest, ranked exception queue."
